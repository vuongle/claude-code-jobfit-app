# Senior Backend Engineer (Payments)

Northgate Pay builds payment infrastructure for Southeast Asian merchants. We process card, wallet and QR payments for more than 12,000 merchants, and we are looking for a senior backend engineer to own critical services in our payment platform.

## Responsibilities

- Design and operate REST APIs for payment authorisation, capture and refund flows.
- Own services end to end: architecture, implementation, deployment and on-call.
- Evolve our event-driven architecture on Kafka and keep checkout and settlement pipelines reliable.
- Work with PostgreSQL and Redis at scale, including query tuning and schema design.
- Manage infrastructure on AWS with Terraform and run workloads on Kubernetes.
- Integrate third-party payment gateways such as Stripe and Adyen, with idempotent flows that are safe to retry.
- Improve CI/CD pipelines and observability for our microservices.

## Requirements

- 5+ years of backend engineering experience, most of it in Python.
- Production experience with FastAPI or Django and strong REST API design.
- Solid PostgreSQL skills, including query tuning and schema design.
- Hands-on production experience with Redis, Docker and Kubernetes.
- Experience running workloads on AWS and managing infrastructure with Terraform.
- Event-driven architecture experience with Kafka or RabbitMQ.
- Experience integrating payment gateways such as Stripe or Adyen.
- Comfortable with CI/CD pipelines (we use GitHub Actions) and microservice observability.

## Nice to have

- Familiarity with PCI DSS compliance.
- Experience with Grafana or Prometheus.
- Experience in the payments or banking industry.
