# Data Scientist

Finlytics helps banks and lenders detect fraud and score credit risk with machine learning. We are looking for a Data Scientist to build and productionise models that run in our decisioning pipeline.

## Responsibilities

- Build, train and evaluate machine learning models for fraud detection and credit scoring.
- Design and analyse A/B experiments with the product team.
- Develop statistical models and hypothesis tests to support risk decisions.
- Productionise models through ML pipelines with Airflow and MLflow.
- Work with cloud infrastructure (AWS or GCP) to deploy and monitor models.
- Present findings and model behaviour to risk and product stakeholders.

## Requirements

- 3+ years of data science experience building predictive models in production.
- Strong Python, including pandas and scikit-learn.
- Solid grounding in machine learning: regression, classification and clustering.
- Experience with a deep learning framework such as TensorFlow or PyTorch.
- Statistical modelling and hypothesis testing.
- Experience designing and analysing A/B tests.
- Experience with workflow and model tooling such as Airflow and MLflow.
- Experience deploying models on AWS or GCP.
- SQL proficiency.

## Nice to have

- MSc or PhD in a quantitative field.
- Experience in fraud or credit risk.
- Experience with feature engineering at scale.
