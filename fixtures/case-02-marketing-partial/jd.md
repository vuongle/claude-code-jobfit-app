# Digital Marketing Manager

Lumen Retail operates e-commerce stores across Vietnam and is hiring a Digital Marketing Manager to own acquisition and retention marketing end to end.

## Responsibilities

- Own the digital marketing roadmap: SEO, SEM, paid social, email and content.
- Lead a team of 4 marketers and agency partners, including hiring and performance reviews.
- Manage a monthly acquisition budget of $50,000 or more and report on channel-level ROI.
- Run marketing automation and lifecycle email programmes in HubSpot, connected to our Salesforce CRM.
- Grow organic traffic through SEO and the content strategy, in partnership with the merchandising team.
- Set up and read A/B tests across landing pages, email and paid creative.
- Report on funnel performance in GA4 and Looker Studio to the leadership team.

## Requirements

- 5+ years in digital marketing, including 2+ years managing a marketing team.
- Proven budget ownership of $50K+ per month, with ROI and cost per acquisition reporting.
- Strong hands-on SEO and SEM, including Google Ads and Meta Ads at scale.
- Experience with marketing automation platforms such as HubSpot or Marketo.
- Experience working with a CRM such as Salesforce.
- Email marketing experience beyond basic newsletters: lifecycle flows, segmentation, deliverability.
- Fluent with GA4 and A/B testing methodology.
- Content strategy experience for e-commerce.

## Nice to have

- Experience with attribution modelling.
- Experience with Looker Studio dashboards.
- Knowledge of the Vietnamese e-commerce market.
